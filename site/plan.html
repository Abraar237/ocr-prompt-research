Prompt Injection Through OCR Document Pipelines

The defence-placement bug: text-channel filters run before OCR output re-enters the model. budget cap $30 (hard stop $25).

Responsible framing. Defensive measurement only. All pipelines are local testbeds; payloads are benign canaries (marker words, format flips, language flips) with programmatic detectors; no harmful instructions, no live systems.

Questions

  1. Canary survival when the injection filter runs before OCR, after OCR, both, or none — and the after-OCR recovery number.
  2. Survival by rendering condition (scan noise, 2° rotation, low DPI) and payload placement (body, footer, margin, watermark, white-on-white, micro-font).
  3. OCR stage: Tesseract vs vision-LLM-as-OCR vs end-to-end vision LLM; does a VLM-as-OCR accidentally "filter" (refuse/omit), and how reliably?
  4. Cost and latency of correct placement.

Pre-registered directions (dated 2026-09-09, before any data)

D1. Before-OCR filters catch ~0% of document-embedded canaries; an after-OCR filter recovers >70% of the miss rate.
D2. VLM-as-OCR transmits fewer injections than Tesseract, but non-deterministically — an accidental, unreliable defence.
D3. Degradations raise OCR WER but do not proportionally reduce injection activation — instructions survive OCR errors better than content does.
D4. Page-furniture placements (footer, watermark) evade the after-OCR filter more than body placement.

Stats: paired bootstrap + permutation clustered by document, interaction logits, BH-FDR over the full family. One analyze.py → results/analysis.json.
