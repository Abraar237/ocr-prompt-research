Deployed placement. Text-channel filters inspect the original request; text rendered inside the scanned image is invisible to them. Measured catch rate on 360 injected documents: 0.0%.
Correct placement. The same filter, moved after the OCR re-entry point, catches the payload in extracted text and recovers 96.9% (Tesseract) / 91.7% (vision-OCR) of the miss — at +1 cheap classifier call.
60 synthetic documents × 6 placements × 6 benign canaries; filters: LLM classifier + regex baseline; models: Gemini flash/pro, GPT-5-mini. All pipelines are local testbeds.
Finding: the filter is not weak — it is standing in the wrong place. Filter placement relative to the OCR re-entry point, not filter quality, decides whether document-embedded prompt injections reach the model.
