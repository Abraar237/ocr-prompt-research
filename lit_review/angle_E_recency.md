## Verdict

**One EXACT-NICHE hit** (Self-Healing OCR Pipelines, ICAART 2026) and **two borderline-EXACT NEAR hits** (Kill-Chain Canaries, 2603.28013; 4D Taxonomy, QPAIN 2026). Self-Healing OCR Pipelines occupies the "prompt-injection defence framework for OCR pipelines (Tesseract + LLM-OCR)" ground and claims first systematic integration; Kill-Chain Canaries independently establishes the canary-tokens-through-pipeline-stages methodology AND the "placement is the highest-leverage decision" conclusion (including whitefont PDF payloads); the 4D taxonomy names pipeline-location as a defence dimension.

**Still apparently open (our residual novelty):** a controlled *measurement* study of filter placement specifically at the OCR re-entry boundary (before-OCR vs after-OCR vs both) with canary *survival curves across rendering conditions* (noise, DPI, rotation, footer/watermark/white-on-white) compared across Tesseract vs vision-LLM-as-OCR vs end-to-end vision LLM. No 2026 paper found does that exact factorial. But the niche is hot and converging from three directions; the framing must explicitly delineate against the three papers above, and speed matters — expect further pre-emption within months.
